Roof Types

Finding the Right Roof Type

Of all roof types, you get reliable protection that is easy on the budget when you choose asphalt shingles. With so many different colors, styles, and prices, it's easy to see why it's the most popular roofing material in Medford and the surrounding area.

No matter what roof type you want to replace — slate, shake, metal, or tile — asphalt roofing can offer you a low-maintenance and affordable alternative. Our roofers are confident we can find a great match for you.

Three-Tab Composition is a popular, standard material that offers a warranty of 20 years or more. It can often be installed in a single day, including tear-off of old materials — a go-to for starter homes, rentals, and quick refreshes.

Architectural Roofing Shingles, also called designer or laminated, cost a bit more but carry a longer warranty. They create a more layered look and longer wear, and serve as a popular, low-cost substitute for slate and cedar shake — the effect of expensive materials at a fraction of the price. Because they're still lightweight, they need no special structural reinforcement.

Even the best roof types can leak if they're put on incorrectly, so hiring the right roof installer is just as important as the materials you choose. Our on-site project manager ensures proper preparation and procedures are carried out, from ice and water shields to double coverage along drip edges. A 15-point system includes roof deck protection and tight flashing in spots prone to leaks.

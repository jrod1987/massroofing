Roofing Materials & Roof Types

Mass Roofing & Gutters is a full-service roofing contractor with expertise across pitched roofs, asphalt and fiberglass shingles, natural slate, wood shingles, flat roofs, composite materials, metal roofing, rubber roofing, and more — plus the gutters and downspouts to match.

Of all roof types, you get reliable protection that's easy on the budget when you choose asphalt shingles. Three-tab composition shingles are a popular, standard material that offer a warranty of 20 years or more and can typically be installed within a day — a great option for starter homes and quick renovations. Architectural (designer or laminated) shingles cost more but offer extended warranties, a more layered look, and longer wear — an affordable alternative to slate and cedar shake that delivers the effect of expensive materials at a fraction of the price.

Ice Dam Repair

Ice dams form when snow melts and refreezes at your roof's edge — it only takes a little ice to back water up under your shingles.

  • Ice Dam Removal
  • Leak & Water Damage Prevention
  • Winter Emergency Response

How Ice Dams Damage Your Home

When snow accumulates on your roof, a cycle of melting and refreezing begins to build a growing block of ice that prevents melting snow from properly running off. Melting snow that puddles up can start to leak back under the roof's shingles, causing expensive damage to ceilings, walls, floors, and even furniture — especially on homes with a low roof slope.
